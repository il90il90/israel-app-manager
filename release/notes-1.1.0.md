- **Android TV, tablets and every other device.** The manager now appears in the Android TV / Google TV launcher and works fully with the remote. A clear frame shows where you are. It starts on the first app and returns to the app you opened, and a long page scrolls to its end. A Refresh button replaces pull-to-refresh, and the edges stay inside the TV's safe area. Keyboards and D-pads work the same way on phones, tablets and Chromebooks.
- **Any app on any device.** An app with no build for your kind of device now installs anyway, using the build made for another one: phone apps on a TV, TV apps on a phone or tablet. Its page says what it was made for. Watch apps are still offered only to watches.
- **Fewer permissions.** The manager no longer asks to see every app on the device, only the apps you can open. Its code is easy to inspect, so Google Play Protect has less reason to warn about it.
- Fixed: on an app's page, a remote or keyboard could not move from the Back button down into the page.
